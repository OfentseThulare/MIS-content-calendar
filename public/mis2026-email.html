Reimagining mining as a force for shared prosperity.

Three days at Sun City bringing mining houses, communities, government, investors, and SMMEs into the same room, for the first time.

20 — 22 July 2026  ·  Sun City
 

Sustainable Harmony and Accountability for Transformation.

The North West Province produces some of the world's most valuable platinum, chrome, and gold. Yet the structural gap between resource extraction and community benefit remains unresolved.

MIS 2026 is the first summit that structurally embeds all five stakeholder groups into a single platform designed to celebrate progress rather than catalogue failure.
